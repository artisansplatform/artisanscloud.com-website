Chatbots in Action

Real-world examples of how AI chatbots provide immediate assistance across the shopper journey.

Order Status

A customer asks, "Has my order shipped?" The chatbot instantly replies with status and tracking links.

When a shopper has a complex inquiry, the chatbot transfers them to a live agent with the full conversation context.
