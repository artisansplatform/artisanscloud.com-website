Why Inventory Management Can Be Challenging

Retailers often struggle to balance stock levels. Without timely insights, stores may face shortages or excess inventory.

Common challenges include:

  • Popular products going out of stock unexpectedly
  • Overstocking slow-moving items
  • Manual monitoring of stock levels
  • Delayed reordering decisions
  • Difficulty predicting demand changes

When inventory isn’t managed proactively, sales opportunities and resources are lost.
