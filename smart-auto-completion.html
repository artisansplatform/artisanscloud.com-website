Why Search Can Slow Down Online Shopping

Many online stores rely on basic search bars that only respond after the full query is typed.

This creates several problems:

  • Customers don’t know the exact product name
  • Typing long queries takes time
  • Spelling mistakes lead to poor results
  • Customers may abandon the search midway
  • Product discovery becomes slow and frustrating

If customers struggle to search, they may leave before finding what they want.
