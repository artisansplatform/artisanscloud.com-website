The Solution

A Structured, AI-Driven Approach to Knowledge Continuity

The Knowledge Harvester uses guided, contextual conversations to capture tacit knowledge — the insights, reasoning and practical know-how that rarely exist in documents. By combining organizational context, project information and role scope, the system builds a searchable knowledge base that teams can access long after transitions occur.

How It Works

01
Define Context

Supervisors or administrators create a project or role context by providing relevant details, documents and focus areas.

02
Capture Knowledge

Employees participate in AI-guided sessions designed to surface critical insights, responsibilities, dependencies, risks and recommendations.

03
Structure & Preserve

The system organizes captured knowledge into a structured repository aligned to the project or role.

04
Enable Access

Authorized team members query the knowledge base using natural language to obtain precise, contextual answers.
