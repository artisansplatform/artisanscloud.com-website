Enterprise Knowledge Is One of the Most Valuable Assets an Organization Owns

Every organization accumulates years of experience, expertise, decisions, processes and operational know-how.

Yet much of this knowledge remains distributed across individuals, teams, projects, documents and business systems.

As organizations grow, knowledge becomes increasingly difficult to locate, maintain and apply consistently. Valuable context is often lost when employees change roles, projects conclude or teams operate across disconnected systems.

The result is slower onboarding, inconsistent decision-making, duplicated effort and reduced operational efficiency.

Vault helps organizations preserve, connect and activate enterprise knowledge so it remains available, relevant and usable across the enterprise.

From Information Storage to Knowledge Intelligence

Most systems are designed to store information. Few are designed to preserve context, connect relationships and make knowledge usable in everyday operations.

Vault enables organizations to move beyond document repositories and traditional knowledge bases by creating a structured enterprise knowledge foundation that reflects how the business actually operates.

Knowledge becomes easier to discover, easier to understand and easier to apply.

Instead of searching for information, teams gain access to relevant knowledge in the context of their work.

Built on an Ontology-Driven Enterprise Knowledge Graph

At the core of Vault is an ontology-driven enterprise knowledge graph.

The knowledge graph connects people, projects, systems, processes, policies and business concepts into a unified knowledge model that reflects the relationships that exist across the enterprise.

Rather than treating knowledge as isolated documents or disconnected records, Vault creates a connected understanding of how information relates across the organization.

This enables enterprises to:

  • Preserve critical business context
  • Connect knowledge across functions and systems
  • Improve knowledge discovery and reuse
  • Establish consistency across teams and locations
  • Create a trusted foundation for enterprise intelligence and decision support

Knowledge is organized around business operations, not file structures.

How Vault Works

Enterprise Knowledge Continuity Across People, Projects and Systems

Critical business knowledge should not depend on individuals or isolated systems. Vault helps organizations retain and transfer knowledge across organizational change.

Across People

Preserve expertise when employees change roles, retire or leave the organization. Maintain continuity without losing critical operational knowledge.

Across Projects

Capture decisions, lessons learned, best practices and outcomes. Ensure project knowledge can be reused across future initiatives.

Across Time

Create a long-term institutional memory that remains accessible, traceable and relevant as the organization evolves.
