Why Inventory Buying Decisions Are So Complex

Retailers need to balance inventory carefully to avoid overstocking or running out of products.

Traditional planning often involves manual calculations across multiple factors, making the process time-consuming and error-prone.

Common challenges include:

  • Predicting future demand accurately
  • Managing forward cover requirements
  • Tracking current stock and pending purchase orders
  • Balancing desired closing stock levels
  • Reacting to seasonal trends and sales fluctuations

When planning isn’t accurate, retailers risk excess inventory, stock shortages, and reduced profitability.

Open-to-Buy Planning in Action

01
Example 1 – Forward Cover Planning

A retailer sets a target for 3 months’ forward cover and enters planned sales for the upcoming quarter. AI considers opening stock, pending purchase orders, and desired closing stock to recommend how much inventory should be purchased.

02
Example 2 – Seasonal Demand Adjustment

Sales for winter jackets increase earlier than expected. AI detects the trend and recommends increasing future purchases before stock runs low.

03
Example 3 – Preventing Overstock

A slow-moving category already has high stock levels and pending purchase orders. AI reduces future buying recommendations to avoid excess inventory.

04
Example 4 – Multi-Category Buying Optimization

AI analyzes sales trends across categories and helps buyers prioritize high-performing products while reducing investment in weaker categories.
