Why One-Size-Fits-All Experiences No Longer Work

Today's customers expect brands to understand their preferences and provide relevant experiences.

However, personalizing experiences for thousands—or even millions—of customers manually is nearly impossible.

Common challenges include

  • Sending the same promotions to every customer
  • Showing identical product recommendations to all shoppers
  • Missing opportunities to engage customers individually
  • Struggling to personalize across multiple channels
  • Limited time and resources to create targeted experiences

When customers receive generic experiences, engagement drops and loyalty becomes harder to build.

Personalized Experiences in Action

Personalized Product Promotions

One customer frequently browses and purchases sneakers, while another prefers formal shoes. Multi-agent AI identifies these preferences and automatically sends a weekend sneaker promotion to the first customer and shares new formal collection arrivals with the second customer. Each customer receives content relevant to their interests.

Channel Preference Optimization

A customer regularly opens mobile app notifications but rarely checks email. AI automatically prioritizes app notifications to maximize engagement.

Personalized Offers

A loyalty member frequently purchases skincare products. AI predicts when they may need a refill and sends a personalized discount at the right moment.

Location-Based Experiences

Customers in colder regions receive recommendations for winter apparel, while customers in warmer climates see seasonal products better suited to their location.

Re-Engaging Inactive Customers

A customer who hasn’t purchased in several months receives a tailored offer featuring products similar to their previous purchases. AI automatically adjusts the message, timing, and channel to increase the likelihood of re-engagement.
