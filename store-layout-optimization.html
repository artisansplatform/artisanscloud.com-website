Why Store Layout Matters More Than You Think

The way products are arranged inside a store has a major impact on shopper behavior. Poor placement can reduce visibility and missed opportunities for sales.

Common challenges include:

  • Popular products being placed in low-traffic areas
  • Customers missing important categories
  • Inefficient use of shelf and floor space
  • Difficulty identifying high-conversion zones
  • Low impulse purchase opportunities

Without smart placement strategies, valuable products may never get noticed.
