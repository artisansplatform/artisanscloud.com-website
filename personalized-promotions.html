Why Generic Promotions Don’t Always Work

Many retailers send the same discounts to everyone, regardless of customer interests or shopping habits.

Common challenges include:

  • Low engagement with broad promotions
  • Discounts reaching uninterested customers
  • Reduced profit margins from unnecessary offers
  • Missed opportunities for targeted marketing
  • Customers ignoring repetitive campaigns

Without personalization, promotions may feel irrelevant and ineffective.
