From connected operations to AI assistance

A shared view of the business gives teams the context to act. A stock shortage can be checked against demand, open orders and availability elsewhere.

We are extending Nexus to help teams interpret this information and identify actions that need attention.

The next step is autonomous commerce

We are building toward agents that carry out defined retail tasks within permissions and business rules. For example, a planned replenishment agent could assess a shortage and propose a stock transfer. Retailers would set approval limits and decide which actions may proceed automatically.

Future connections with Vault will add operating experience, such as why a store keeps extra stock or how teams handle recurring exceptions.
