Why Shoppers Leave Without Buying

Not every shopper finds something that catches their attention. When product suggestions are generic, customers may leave without exploring further.

This often happens because

  • Products shown are not relevant to the shopper
  • No complementary items are suggested
  • Shoppers don’t discover additional options
  • Same recommendations are shown to everyone
  • Lack of personalized shopping experience

When customers don’t feel understood, they leave without buying.
