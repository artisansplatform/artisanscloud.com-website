Enterprise AI Products

Three independent products. One shared objective: better outcomes across your organization.

Arena

Workforce Readiness Through Role-Play and Simulation

Arena enables organizations to prepare employees for customer interactions, operational scenarios and role-specific responsibilities through simulation-based learning experiences.

From onboarding and workforce readiness to customer engagement and leadership development, Arena helps teams build confidence, consistency and preparedness before live execution.

  • Role-based simulation and practice
  • Scenario-driven learning experiences
  • Workforce onboarding and readiness
  • Performance assessment and feedback
  • Customer service and sales enablement

Vault

Knowledge Intelligence for the Enterprise

Critical enterprise knowledge often resides across documents, systems and individual expertise. Vault helps organizations capture, organize and activate institutional knowledge so it remains accessible and usable across the enterprise.

Designed for knowledge-intensive environments, Vault provides a structured foundation for knowledge discovery, continuity and organizational learning.

  • Enterprise knowledge capture
  • Enterprise knowledge Graph
  • Knowledge discovery and retrieval
  • Institutional knowledge preservation
  • Governance and Knowledge activation across teams

Lumen

Enterprise Copilot for Knowledge and Decision Support

Lumen provides conversational access to enterprise knowledge, operational information and business context.

Designed to support decision-making and execution, Lumen helps employees access relevant information, navigate processes and interact with enterprise systems through a natural and intuitive experience.

  • Conversational enterprise assistance

  • Operational decision support
  • Workflow guidance
  • Context-aware information retrieval
